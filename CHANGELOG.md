## [1.0.0]

- First release: a magic lantern that deals slides of painted glass — hero with a job, place, magic thing with a power, trouble, helper, quirk — for making up bedtime tales together.
- Shadow-puppet cut-outs after Lotte Reiniger: 42 heroes, 28 jobs, 34 places, 48 magic things, and signs for troubles, twists and endings.
- Telling face down, twists when the phone is turned over, a candle timer, turns round the table, the grown-up's hints after Propp, recording.
- The quiet ending with night sounds made on the phone; the shelf, sequels, the world map, old friends, parcels of rare glass, painted slides, shared evenings by code, a printable book and the morning widget. English and Russian.
